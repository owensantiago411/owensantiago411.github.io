About

An Energetic Mind,
A Focused Purpose.

Despite the challenges of ADHD, I put everything I've got into my work. Because if work isn't done well, then what's the point?

I'm a Filipino-American with a white mother from the South and an immigrant father from the Philippines who lived most of his life in California. Despite childhood challenges on both sides, my parents have given me great opportunities that I will use to better the world via MRI.

In my mind, every problem in my way isn't a boulder in my path, but rather a stepping stone to success. When I struggled to understand something in school, I just worked harder. In Intro to Anatomy & Physiology at the local high school, I found learning tough. But I worked hard and made it work, and I ended up getting A's in both semesters. Perseverance — even when I have no background experience on the subject — is a superpower of mine.

Whatever I need to do, I will get done. I am extremely passionate about anything I do, and will work until whatever I do is as good as it can be.
