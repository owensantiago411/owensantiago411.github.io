About

An Energetic Mind,
A Focused Purpose.

I am an aspiring Radiologic Technologist driven by a deep commitment to patient-centered care and technical excellence. Growing up as a Filipino-American with parents from diverse backgrounds, I learned early on the value of treating every individual with respect and compassion—values that will shape how I care for my patients.

Living with ADHD has sharpened my ability to notice details others might miss—a critical skill for precise image evaluation and clinical problem-solving. In demanding courses like Anatomy & Physiology, I combined that attention to detail with relentless effort and earned top grades through perseverance alone.

Whether it's ensuring safety protocols are followed, communicating clearly with patients, or refining my technical skills, I bring the same standard to everything I do: get it done right, every time.
