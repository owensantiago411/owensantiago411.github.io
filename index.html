About

An Energetic Mind,
A Focused Purpose.

I am an aspiring Radiologic Technologist driven by a deep commitment to patient-centered care and technical excellence. Growing up as a Filipino-American with parents from diverse backgrounds, I learned early on the profound value of bias-free compassion and treating every individual with respect. These values form the foundation of how I intend to care for my patients.

My journey hasn't been without challenges, but I view obstacles as stepping stones. Living with ADHD has uniquely honed my ability to notice critical details that others might miss—a vital skill when it comes to precise image evaluation and problem-solving in clinical settings. In demanding courses like Anatomy & Physiology, I combined this natural attention to detail with an unrelenting work ethic, securing top grades through pure perseverance.

Whether it's ensuring safety protocols are meticulously followed, communicating clearly with patients to ease their anxiety, or continuously refining my technical skills, I am dedicated to professionalism. Whatever the task requires, I will see it through with integrity and passion.
